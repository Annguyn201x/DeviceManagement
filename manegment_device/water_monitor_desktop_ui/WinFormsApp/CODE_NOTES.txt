WATER MONITOR WINFORMS - GHI CHU CODE
====================================

1. Program.cs
   Entry point cua ung dung. ApplicationConfiguration.Initialize() khoi tao
   giao dien WinForms, sau do mo MainForm.

2. MainForm.cs - MainForm
   Cua so chinh va dieu huong 4 man hinh. BuildShell() tao thanh ben va
   thanh tieu de; SetPage() xoa noi dung cu truoc khi hien thi man moi.

   ShowOverview()
   Hien thi chi so pH, TDS, nhiet do va TrendPreview. So lieu la minh hoa;
   thay bang gia tri tu backend khi co API/MQTT.

   ShowHistory()
   Man hinh lich su dang la bo loc mau. Can ket noi API/backend de lay va
   ve du lieu thuc te.

   ShowDevices()
   Hien thi danh sach tu BindingList<WaterDevice>. Nut Them thiet bi mo
   AddDevice(); nut Hieu chinh mo Calibrate() cho dong dang chon.

   AddDevice()
   Kiem tra ten, ID bat buoc va khong cho phep ID bi trung. Thiet bi moi
   chi duoc them vao bo nho cua phien hien tai, chua luu vao database.

   Calibrate()
   Nhan do lech pH, he so TDS va do lech nhiet do. So duoc chap nhan theo
   dau cham hoac dau phay; he so TDS phai lon hon 0. Day chua phat lenh MQTT.
   Khi co backend, thay phan MessageBox thanh lenh publish theo topic/thong
   diep ma firmware quy uoc.

   ShowSettings()
   Vi tri de bo sung dia chi broker, cong, client ID va tuy chon ket noi.
   Khong hard-code thong tin dang nhap hay broker trong ban giao dien mau.

3. WaterDevice
   Model nho cho danh sach xem thu: ten, ma/MAC, khu vuc va trang thai.
   Nen thay doi model nay theo schema cua backend sau khi thong nhat API.

4. TrendPreview
   Ve duong xu huong bang GDI+ de khong can thu vien bieu do. Du lieu hien
   tai la diem tinh; co the thay bang chuoi mau do tu backend.

5. WaterMonitor.WinForms.csproj
   Project Windows Forms, target .NET 8 Windows. Chay tu thu muc goc:
   dotnet run --project .\WinFormsApp\WaterMonitor.WinForms.csproj

LUU Y
-----
- Ung dung duoc dinh huong chay tren may Windows trong mang noi bo.
- Chua co MQTT, backend, database, dang nhap that hoac du lieu thoi gian thuc.
- Khong xem thong bao "da kiem tra" la xac nhan thiet bi da nhan cau hinh.
- Flutter source o thu muc goc la prototype cu, khong duoc WinForms build.
